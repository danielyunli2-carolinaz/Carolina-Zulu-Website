Behind every flight school and every flight club lies one thing: our shared community. As young men and women, with many being newcomers into the aviation space, we understand how important that community truly is. So be it a group trip to the beach, a party after a check ride pass, or just a simple group of lifelong friends - we hope you can find that community with us!
